29 CFR 1926.451 establishes general requirements for scaffold capacity, platform construction, fall protection, access, and safe use to prevent structural failure and falls during construction work.

  ['29 CFR §1926.451(g)(1)'] Each employee on a scaffold more than 10 feet (3.1 m) above a lower level shall be protected from falling to that lower level....
  ['29 CFR §1926.451(g)(4)(i)'] Guardrail systems shall be installed along all open sides and ends of platforms....
  ['29 CFR §1926.451(h)(1)'] In addition to wearing hardhats each employee on a scaffold shall be provided with additional protection from falling hand tools, debris, and other small objects through the installation of toeboards, screens, or guardrail systems, or through the erection of debris nets, catch platforms, or canopy structures that contain or deflect the falling objects....
  ['29 CFR §1926.451(f)(3)'] Scaffolds and scaffold components shall be inspected for visible defects by a competent person before each work shift, and after any occurrence which could affect a scaffold's structural integrity....
  ['29 CFR §1926.451(f)(7)'] Scaffolds shall be erected, moved, dismantled, or altered only under the supervision and direction of a competent person qualified in scaffold erection, moving, dismantling or alteration....

These requirements prevent injuries by ensuring scaffolds can support workers and materials without collapsing (4:1 safety factor), preventing falls from elevation through guardrails and fall arrest systems for work above 10 feet, and protecting workers below from falling objects through toeboards and debris nets. Regular inspections by competent persons identify structural defects before failure occurs, and proper supervision ensures scaffolds are erected and used safely.
