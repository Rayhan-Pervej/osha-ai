Title: § 1919.7 Voluntary amendment or termination of accreditation.
Section: 1919.7
Path: PART 1919—GEAR CERTIFICATION
 > PART 1919—GEAR CERTIFICATION

The accreditation of any person may be voluntarily amended or terminated upon written request filed with the Assistant Secretary.
