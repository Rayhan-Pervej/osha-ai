Title: § 1921.11 Postponement or change of place of hearing.
Section: 1921.11
Path: PART 1921—RULES OF PRACTICE IN ENFORCEMENT PROCEEDINGS UNDER SECTION 41 OF THE LONGSHOREMEN'S AND HARBOR WORKERS' COMPENSATION ACT

If in the judgment of the hearing examiner convenience or necessity so requires, he may postpone the time or change the place of the hearing.
